## 1. Purpose
This policy sets out how staff apply for and take annual leave.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to all permanent staff.

## 3. Definitions
- **Annual leave:** paid leave for rest, separate from other types of leave.
- **Line manager:** the manager the staff member reports to.

## 4. Roles and responsibilities
- **Staff member:** applies for leave on the paper leave form.
- **Line manager:** signs the leave form.

## 5. Procedure
### 5.1 Entitlement
Permanent staff are entitled to 28 working days of annual leave per calendar year.

### 5.2 Applying for leave
Apply for annual leave at least 14 days before the first day of leave, using the paper leave form signed by your line manager.

### 5.3 Carrying leave forward
Up to 10 unused days may be carried into the next year. They must be taken by 31 March.

## 6. Exceptions and escalation
- **Emergency leave:** discuss with your line manager.

## Appendix A: Frequently asked questions
### Q1. How early must I apply for annual leave?
At least 14 days before the first day of leave, on the paper leave form.
