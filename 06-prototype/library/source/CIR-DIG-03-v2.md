## 1. Purpose
This circular sets out how branch staff enrol customers for SIM banking, so that only the account holder can operate the account from their phone.

## 2. Scope
This circular applies to individual customers with active savings or current accounts.

## 3. Definitions
- **SIM banking:** banking services on a mobile phone through the bank's USSD menu.
- **SIM banking PIN:** the secret number the customer uses to confirm SIM banking transactions.
- **SIM registration:** the record held by the mobile network showing whose name a SIM card is registered in.
- **Form SB-01:** the SIM banking enrolment form.

## 4. Roles and responsibilities
- **Customer service officer:** verifies the customer and phone ownership and completes the enrolment.
- **Supervisor:** authorises the enrolment.
- **Digital Banking:** supports the service and handles blocks reported through the contact centre.

## 5. Procedure
### 5.1 Verify the customer's identity [MANDATORY CONTROL]
Check the customer's original identity document against the account record.

### 5.2 Confirm phone ownership [MANDATORY CONTROL]
Confirm that the SIM card is registered in the customer's own name, using the network's SIM registration check. Do not enrol a phone number registered to another person.

### 5.3 Enrolment form
The customer completes and signs Form SB-01 with the account number and phone number.

### 5.4 Link the account and set limits
Link the account to the phone number on the SIM banking system. The default daily limit is TZS 2,000,000. Higher limits need the branch manager's approval.

### 5.5 The customer sets the PIN privately [MANDATORY CONTROL]
The customer sets the SIM banking PIN on their own phone. Staff must never ask for, see or write down a customer's PIN.

### 5.6 Test transaction
Ask the customer to make a balance enquiry to confirm the service works.

### 5.7 Supervisor authorisation
The supervisor checks Form SB-01 and authorises the enrolment.

## 6. Exceptions and escalation
- **Lost or stolen phone:** the customer reports it at a branch or through the contact centre; SIM banking is blocked immediately.
- **Forgotten PIN:** the customer visits a branch with an original identity document for a PIN reset.

## Appendix A: Frequently asked questions
### Q1. Can I enrol a customer whose phone is registered in their spouse's name?
No. The SIM card must be registered in the customer's own name.

### Q2. What is the daily SIM banking limit?
TZS 2,000,000 by default. Higher limits need the branch manager's approval.
