## 1. Purpose
This policy sets out the standards of conduct expected of every staff member.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to all staff, contractors and interns.

## 3. Definitions
- **Conflict of interest:** a situation where personal interests could affect a staff member's decisions.
- **Relative:** a spouse, parent, child, sibling or in-law.
- **Gift register:** the branch register where gifts are declared.

## 4. Roles and responsibilities
- **Staff member:** follows this policy and declares conflicts and gifts.
- **Line manager:** ensures their team follows this policy.
- **Compliance:** advises on conflicts and reviews gift declarations.

## 5. Procedure
### 5.1 Conflicts of interest
Declare any conflict of interest to your line manager in writing as soon as it arises, and once a year on the annual declaration.

### 5.2 Own and relatives' transactions [MANDATORY CONTROL]
Staff must not process, approve or authorise transactions on their own accounts or the accounts of relatives. Pass them to another officer.

### 5.3 Gifts and hospitality
Gifts of up to TZS 100,000 may be accepted and recorded in the gift register. Gifts above TZS 100,000 must be declined or declared to Compliance. Cash gifts are never accepted.

### 5.4 Confidentiality
Customer and bank information may be used only for work and shared only with people who need it for their work.

### 5.5 Social media
Do not post customer information, internal documents or photographs of bank premises on social media.

### 5.6 Speaking up
Report suspected fraud or misconduct through the whistleblowing line. Reports may be anonymous and retaliation is not tolerated.

## 6. Exceptions and escalation
- **Unsure whether a gift or activity is allowed:** ask Compliance before accepting.

## Appendix A: Frequently asked questions
### Q1. Can I process a deposit to my own account?
No. Pass your own and relatives' transactions to another officer.

### Q2. What is the gift limit?
TZS 100,000. Cash gifts are never accepted.
