## 1. Purpose
This circular sets out how branch staff open accounts for individual customers and complete know-your-customer (KYC) checks.

## 2. Scope
This circular covers savings and current accounts for individuals aged 18 and over, and accounts for minors operated by a parent or guardian.

## 3. Definitions
- **KYC:** know your customer; the checks that establish who the customer is and the risk they present.
- **Politically exposed person (PEP):** a person who holds or has held a prominent public position, and their close family and associates.
- **Customer risk rating:** the low, medium or high rating recorded in core banking for every customer.
- **Enhanced due diligence (EDD):** additional checks and approvals required for high-risk customers.

## 4. Roles and responsibilities
- **Customer service officer:** identifies the customer, screens, risk-rates and opens the account.
- **Supervisor:** authorises the account after checking the file.
- **Compliance:** carries out EDD and approves high-risk customers.
- **Back office quality assurance (QA):** reviews every new account file.

## 5. Procedure
### 5.1 Identify the customer [MANDATORY CONTROL]
Ask for an original identity document: national ID card, passport or driving licence. Record the document number and check that the photograph matches the customer. Expired documents are not accepted.

### 5.2 Proof of address
Take proof of residential address issued within the last three months: a utility bill, a tenancy agreement or a letter from the local government office.

### 5.3 Photograph and specimen signature
Capture the customer's photograph and specimen signature in core banking.

### 5.4 Screen the customer [MANDATORY CONTROL]
Screen the customer against the sanctions, watch and PEP lists. Stop and refer to Compliance if there is a possible match.

### 5.5 Risk-rate the customer [MANDATORY CONTROL]
Complete the customer risk rating in core banking, based on occupation, expected account activity, source of funds and screening results.

### 5.6 Enhanced due diligence for high-risk customers [MANDATORY CONTROL]
If the rating is high, or the customer is a PEP, send the file to Compliance. Do not open the account until Compliance approves in writing.

### 5.7 Open the account
Open the account in core banking. The supervisor authorises it after checking the file.

### 5.8 Submit the file to quality assurance
Submit the complete file to back office QA within one working day of opening.

## 6. Exceptions and escalation
- **Customers without proof of address:** a basic account with transaction limits may be opened with a letter from the local government office.
- **Minors:** the parent or guardian provides their own identity document and the child's birth certificate.

## Appendix A: Frequently asked questions
### Q1. Can I open an account with an expired passport?
No. Only valid original identity documents are accepted.

### Q2. What if the customer is a politically exposed person?
Follow section 5.6: send the file to Compliance and wait for written approval.
