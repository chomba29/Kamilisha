## 1. Purpose
This policy sets out how staff plan, apply for and take annual leave, so that staff rest and branches keep enough cover to serve customers safely.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to all permanent staff. Staff on fixed-term contracts follow the leave terms in their contracts.

## 3. Definitions
- **Annual leave:** paid leave for rest, separate from sick, maternity, paternity and compassionate leave.
- **Line manager:** the manager the staff member reports to; for branch staff, usually the branch manager or supervisor.
- **HR system:** the bank's human resources system used to apply for and approve leave.
- **Authorised signatory:** a staff member authorised to approve transactions above teller limits.
- **Leave year:** the calendar year from 1 January to 31 December.

## 4. Roles and responsibilities
- **Staff member:** plans leave early and applies in the HR system.
- **Line manager:** approves or declines leave in the HR system and keeps enough cover.
- **Branch manager:** confirms branch cover before approving leave for branch staff.
- **Human Resources:** maintains leave balances and this policy.

## 5. Procedure
### 5.1 Entitlement
Permanent staff are entitled to 28 working days of annual leave per leave year. Staff joining during the year receive a proportional entitlement.

### 5.2 Applying for leave
Apply for annual leave in the HR system at least 7 days before the first day of leave. Your line manager approves or declines the request in the HR system. Paper leave forms are no longer accepted.

### 5.3 Approval and branch cover [MANDATORY CONTROL]
Before approving leave for branch staff, the branch manager confirms that the branch keeps at least two authorised signatories on duty on every day of the leave.

### 5.4 Carrying leave forward
Up to 5 unused days may be carried into the next leave year. They must be taken by 31 March, after which they lapse. Unused leave is not paid out except when a staff member leaves the bank.

### 5.5 Changing or cancelling approved leave
To change or cancel approved leave, submit the change in the HR system before the leave starts. Your line manager approves the change.

### 5.6 Public holidays during leave
Public holidays that fall during approved annual leave are not counted as leave days.

### 5.7 Recall from leave
In an emergency, the branch manager may recall a staff member from leave with Human Resources' approval. The days not taken are restored to the staff member's balance.

## 6. Exceptions and escalation
- **Less than 7 days' notice:** possible only for emergencies, with the line manager's approval recorded in the HR system.
- **HR system unavailable:** email the request to your line manager and enter it in the HR system when it is available.

## Appendix A: Frequently asked questions
### Q1. How early must I apply for annual leave?
At least 7 days before the first day of leave, in the HR system.

### Q2. How many unused days can I carry into next year?
Up to 5 days, to be taken by 31 March.

### Q3. Can I still use the paper leave form?
No. From version 2 all leave is applied for in the HR system.
