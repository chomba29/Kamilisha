## 1. Purpose
This policy sets out how staff apply for salary advances and staff loans.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to confirmed permanent staff.

## 3. Definitions
- **Salary advance:** part of next month's salary paid early and recovered from that salary.
- **Staff loan:** a loan to a staff member on staff terms, repaid by salary deduction.
- **Net salary:** salary after tax and statutory deductions.

## 4. Roles and responsibilities
- **Staff member:** applies in the HR system.
- **Human Resources:** checks eligibility and limits.
- **Finance:** approves and pays.

## 5. Procedure
### 5.1 Salary advance
Confirmed staff may request a salary advance of up to 50 percent of net monthly salary, once per quarter. It is recovered in full from the next payroll.

### 5.2 Staff loans
Confirmed staff may apply for a staff loan with a term of up to 5 years. Total deductions for all staff loans may not exceed 40 percent of net monthly salary.

### 5.3 Applying
Apply in the HR system. Human Resources checks eligibility and limits; Finance approves and pays.

### 5.4 No self-approval [MANDATORY CONTROL]
Staff must not approve, process or disburse their own salary advance or loan, or those of a relative.

## 6. Exceptions and escalation
- **Staff on probation:** not eligible, except for emergencies approved by the Head of Human Resources.

## Appendix A: Frequently asked questions
### Q1. How much salary advance can I take?
Up to 50 percent of net monthly salary, once per quarter.

### Q2. What is the longest staff loan term?
5 years.
