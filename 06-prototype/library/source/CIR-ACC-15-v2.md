## 1. Purpose
This circular sets out how branch staff update customer information such as address, phone number, name and business signatories, without opening the door to account takeover fraud.

## 2. Scope
This circular applies to individual and business customers at all branches.

## 3. Definitions
- **Customer update form (Form CU-01):** the form on which the customer requests a change to their information.
- **Maker-checker:** one staff member captures a change and a second staff member authorises it.
- **Registered phone number:** the number used for SMS alerts, SIM banking and one-time passwords.

## 4. Roles and responsibilities
- **Customer service officer (maker):** verifies the customer and captures the change.
- **Supervisor (checker):** authorises the change after checking the documents.
- **Back office quality assurance (QA):** samples completed updates.

## 5. Procedure
### 5.1 Verify the customer's identity [MANDATORY CONTROL]
Check the customer's original identity document against the account record before accepting any change.

### 5.2 Customer update form
The customer completes and signs the customer update form (Form CU-01), stating each change requested.

### 5.3 Supporting documents by type of change
- Address: proof of address issued within the last three months.
- Name: marriage certificate, deed poll or court order.
- Business signatories: a board resolution (companies) or a letter signed by all partners (partnerships), with the new signatories' identity documents.

### 5.4 Change of registered phone number [MANDATORY CONTROL]
A change of registered phone number is accepted only in person at the branch, with an original identity document. It is never accepted by phone, email or letter, because the number receives one-time passwords. After the change, the customer re-registers for SIM banking under CIR-DIG-03.

### 5.5 Capture and authorise the change [MANDATORY CONTROL]
The officer captures the change in core banking and a supervisor authorises it (maker-checker). No one may authorise their own capture.

### 5.6 Notify the customer
Core banking sends an SMS confirming the change. For a phone number change, the SMS goes to both the old and the new number.

## 6. Exceptions and escalation
- **Customer cannot visit:** changes other than phone number may be made by an attorney holding a valid power of attorney, with branch manager approval.
- **Suspected impersonation:** stop, keep the documents and inform Fraud Risk the same day.

## Appendix A: Frequently asked questions
### Q1. Can a customer change their phone number by calling the contact centre?
No. Phone number changes are made only in person at a branch with an original identity document.

### Q2. Who authorises a change of signatories on a company account?
A supervisor authorises it in core banking after checking the board resolution and the new signatories' identity documents.
