## 1. Purpose
This circular sets out how branch staff enrol customers for internet banking and reset internet banking passwords safely.

## 2. Scope
This circular covers individual and business customers.

## 3. Definitions
- **Internet banking:** banking services through the bank's website.
- **Temporary password:** a one-time password sent by SMS to the registered phone number.
- **Maker and checker roles:** for business customers, one user captures payments and another approves them.

## 4. Roles and responsibilities
- **Customer service officer:** verifies the customer and completes enrolment or reset.
- **Supervisor:** authorises enrolments and resets.
- **Digital Banking:** manages the internet banking platform.

## 5. Procedure
### 5.1 Verify the customer's identity [MANDATORY CONTROL]
Check the original identity document of the customer, or of every business user, against the account record.

### 5.2 Enrolment form
The customer completes Form IB-01. Business customers attach a board resolution or partners' letter naming each user and their maker or checker role.

### 5.3 Set up users and roles
Create the user profiles. For business customers, no single user may both capture and approve payments.

### 5.4 Temporary password
The system sends a temporary password by SMS to the registered phone number. It expires after 24 hours and must be changed at first login.

### 5.5 Password reset in person only [MANDATORY CONTROL]
Password resets are done only in person at a branch with an original identity document. Never reset a password on a request by phone, email or social media.

### 5.6 Supervisor authorisation
The supervisor checks the form and authorises the enrolment or reset.

## 6. Exceptions and escalation
- **Registered phone number out of date:** update it first under CIR-ACC-15.
- **Suspected account takeover:** block internet banking and inform Fraud Risk the same day.

## Appendix A: Frequently asked questions
### Q1. Can I reset a customer's password if they call and answer security questions?
No. Resets are done only in person with an original identity document.

### Q2. How long is the temporary password valid?
24 hours.
