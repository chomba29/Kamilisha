## 1. Purpose
This circular sets out how branch staff document, register, store and release loan collateral.

## 2. Scope
This circular covers land and buildings, motor vehicles, cash cover and guarantees.

## 3. Definitions
- **Collateral:** an asset pledged to secure a loan.
- **Charge:** the legal registration of the bank's interest in the collateral.
- **Approved valuer:** a valuer on the bank's approved panel.
- **Loss payee:** the party paid by the insurer if the asset is lost or damaged.

## 4. Roles and responsibilities
- **Relationship officer:** collects collateral documents and arranges valuation.
- **Credit administration:** registers charges, checks insurance and keeps original documents.
- **Branch manager:** approves collateral release with credit administration.

## 5. Procedure
### 5.1 Acceptable collateral
Land and buildings with a title deed, motor vehicles with a registration card, cash cover held in the bank, and guarantees from acceptable guarantors.

### 5.2 Valuation
Land and buildings are valued by an approved valuer. The valuation report must be less than 24 months old at approval.

### 5.3 Registration of the charge
Credit administration registers the bank's charge with the relevant registry before disbursement.

### 5.4 Insurance
Collateral is insured with the bank named as loss payee. Credit administration keeps a copy of the policy and tracks renewals.

### 5.5 Custody of original documents [MANDATORY CONTROL]
Original title deeds and registration cards are kept in the fire-proof safe under dual control. Every movement in or out is recorded in the security register.

### 5.6 Release of collateral [MANDATORY CONTROL]
Collateral is released only after full repayment is confirmed by credit administration and the release is approved by the branch manager. Originals are handed only to the customer, against signature.

## 6. Exceptions and escalation
- **Collateral in another person's name:** the owner must sign a third-party charge with independent legal advice.
- **Lost original document:** report to the branch manager and Legal the same day.

## Appendix A: Frequently asked questions
### Q1. How old can a valuation report be?
Less than 24 months old at approval.

### Q2. Who keeps the original title deed?
Credit administration, in the fire-proof safe under dual control.
