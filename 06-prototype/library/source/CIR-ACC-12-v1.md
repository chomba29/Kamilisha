## 1. Purpose
This circular sets out how branch staff open current accounts for businesses.

## 2. Scope
This circular covers sole proprietors, partnerships and limited companies.

## 3. Definitions
- **Signatory:** a person authorised to operate the account.
- **TIN certificate:** the certificate showing the business's tax identification number.

## 4. Roles and responsibilities
- **Customer service or relationship officer:** checks documents, verifies signatories, screens the customer and opens the account.
- **Compliance:** carries out enhanced due diligence for high-risk customers.

## 5. Procedure
### 5.1 Required documents [MANDATORY CONTROL]
Every business account needs a valid business licence, a tax identification number (TIN) certificate and the original identity documents of the owners and signatories. Partnerships also provide the partnership deed. Companies also provide the certificate of incorporation, the memorandum and articles of association and a board resolution to open the account.

### 5.2 Verify owners and signatories [MANDATORY CONTROL]
Verify the original identity document of every owner and signatory and take their specimen signatures.

### 5.3 Screen and risk-rate the customer [MANDATORY CONTROL]
Screen the business and every owner and signatory against the sanctions and watch lists, then complete the customer risk rating.

### 5.4 Enhanced due diligence [MANDATORY CONTROL]
If the risk rating is high, send the file to Compliance for enhanced due diligence. Do not open the account until Compliance approves.

### 5.5 Open the account
Open the account and record the mandate in core banking. Submit the file to back office QA.

## 6. Exceptions and escalation
- **Missing document:** do not open the account until all documents are provided.

## Appendix A: Frequently asked questions
### Q1. Does a sole proprietor need a business licence?
Yes. Every business account needs a valid business licence.
