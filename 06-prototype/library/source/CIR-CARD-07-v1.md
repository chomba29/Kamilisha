## 1. Purpose
This circular sets out how branch staff replace a customer's debit card when the card is lost, stolen, damaged or retained by an ATM, so that every branch handles replacements in the same way.

## 2. Scope
This circular applies to all Mfano Bank branches and to all debit cards issued on current and savings accounts. It does not cover credit cards or prepaid cards. It applies to customer service officers, tellers, supervisors, the card centre and back office quality assurance.

## 3. Definitions
- **Block reference:** the reference number the card system issues when a card is blocked.
- **Card system:** the bank system used to block cards and capture replacement requests.
- **Replacement request:** the request captured on the card system for a new card to be produced.
- **Card collection register:** the branch register in which customers sign when they collect a card.
- **Retained card:** a card captured by an ATM.

## 4. Roles and responsibilities
- **Customer service officer:** verifies the customer, blocks the old card, captures the replacement request and hands over the new card.
- **Supervisor:** authorises the replacement request.
- **Card centre:** produces the new card and PIN and dispatches them to the branch.
- **Back office quality assurance (QA):** reviews replacement files.

## 5. Procedure
### 5.1 Verify the customer's identity [MANDATORY CONTROL]
Ask for an original identity document: national ID card, passport or driving licence. Compare the photograph and the signature with the account records before any other step.

### 5.2 Block the old card [MANDATORY CONTROL]
Block the old card immediately on the card system. A blocking form is not required. Record the block reference in the replacement request.

### 5.3 Capture the replacement request and fee
Capture the replacement request on the card system and charge the replacement fee of TZS 10,000. Waive the fee for damaged cards less than 12 months old.

### 5.4 Supervisor authorisation
The supervisor authorises the replacement request on the card system.

### 5.5 Card production and dispatch
The card centre produces the new card within five working days and sends the card and the PIN mailer to the branch in separate packages.

### 5.6 Hand over the new card [MANDATORY CONTROL]
When the new card arrives, check the customer's original identity document again and ask the customer to sign the card collection register before handing over the card.

## 6. Exceptions and escalation
- **Transactions after the loss:** if the customer reports transactions they did not make, raise a card dispute and inform Fraud Risk.
- **Card system unavailable:** block the card through the contact centre hotline and record the call reference.

## Appendix A: Officer checklist
- [ ] Original identity document checked
- [ ] Card blocked on the card system
- [ ] Block reference recorded in the replacement request
- [ ] Replacement request captured and fee charged or waived

## Appendix B: Frequently asked questions
### Q1. Do I need a form to block a lost card?
No. Block the card directly on the card system and record the block reference.

### Q2. Is the fee charged for a damaged card?
The fee is waived for damaged cards less than 12 months old. Otherwise TZS 10,000 applies.
