# Pain-point log

Eight pain points from the discovery interview. Frequency and severity are the practitioner's ratings; severity reflects the control or money at risk.

| ID | Pain point | Effect | Control at risk | Frequency | Severity | Map |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | Update emails are missed | Staff keep working from the old version of a circular | Any step that changed | High | High | 1, 2 |
| P2 | No quick way to tell which version is current | Colleagues answer from memory or habit; two officers give one customer different answers | Any step that changed | High | High | 1, 2, 3 |
| P3 | Errors are found late | QA returns, audit queries months later, or fraud loss when nothing catches the error | All | Medium | High | 1, 2, 3 |
| P4 | Card-blocking form skipped on card replacement | Lost card keeps transacting; bank takes the loss | Signed blocking form | Medium | High | 2 |
| P5 | Business account documents list applied inconsistently | Wasted customer trips, QA returns, KYC gaps | Documents check | High | Medium | 3 |
| P6 | Slow to find the right section | Officer gives up on the intranet and asks a colleague | None directly | High | Medium | 1 |
| P7 | Circulars are long, in English and full of jargon | Slow reading; misreading under time pressure | None directly | High | Medium | 1 |
| P8 | Training unit cannot see where staff struggle | Training is generic, not aimed at the procedures causing errors | None directly | Ongoing | Medium | — |

## Evidence behind each

- **P1, P2:** Changes are announced by email; missed emails lead to answers *kwa mazoea* (from habit). Example: a business licence required one day and not the next.
- **P3, P4:** A skipped blocking form is caught by QA, later raised as an audit query, or, worst case, the card transacts and the bank absorbs a fraud loss.
- **P5:** Account opening is one of the two procedures named as most confusing, because its documents list changes.
- **P6, P7:** Three layers of effort: find the circular, find the section in 20–50 pages, then read it.
- **P8:** No record exists of which questions staff ask, so the training unit cannot see recurring gaps.

## What happens next

Stage 2 decides the right fix for each pain point. Not every one needs AI: some need a process change (a "what changed" box at the top of each circular), some a mandatory checklist, some a report.
