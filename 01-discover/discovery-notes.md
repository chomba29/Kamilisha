# Discovery notes

**Session:** practitioner interview, October 2026
**Participant:** former branch staff, nine years across operations, credit, relationship management and acting branch leadership (roles only, no names)
**Method:** semi-structured, one question at a time; decisions agreed with the participant at the end

## Findings

1. All circulars, procedures and staff policies sit on the official intranet, updated whenever something changes.
2. Finding an answer takes three steps: find the right circular, find the section in 20–50 pages, read it. (P6, P7)
3. That effort pushes staff to ask a colleague or supervisor instead. (P6)
4. Changes are announced by email. Missed emails mean answers come from an old version or from habit, *kwa mazoea*. (P1, P2)
5. Card replacement and account opening are the most confusing, because they change most often. Example: a licence required one day, not the next. (P4, P5)
6. Result: two officers give the same customer different answers, such as whether a blocking form is needed or whether a licence is required. (P2)
7. A skipped card-blocking form is caught by QA, later by an audit query, or not at all, and the card transacts and the bank takes a fraud loss. (P3, P4)

## Decisions agreed

| Topic | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Users | All branch staff; the training unit as a second user |
| Answer style | Short steps that beat reading the intranet, with circular, section and version cited underneath |
| Language | Plain English or Swahili, because the circulars are English with technical jargon |
| When unsure | One line: "Not found in the current circulars. Ask your supervisor." It never invents an answer |
| Question log | Every question is logged; the training unit gets a most-asked dashboard |
| Demo library | Three fictional circulars: card replacement, business account opening, staff leave. Each has an old and a current version, so the demo shows the current answer where habit gives the old one |
| Success measures | QA returns first (fast signal), then service time, then audit queries (slower) |

## Assumptions to confirm

- **Card replacement order** (identity check, then signed blocking form, then system block) is a fictional design for the demo, modelled on common branch practice.
- **The licence rule change** (v1: licence for every business account; v2: sole proprietors may give a tax registration certificate instead) is fictional, created to demonstrate version conflict.
- **Frequency and severity ratings** are one practitioner's judgement; Stage 4 replaces them with baseline numbers.
