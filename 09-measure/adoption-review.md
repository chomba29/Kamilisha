# Adoption review: week 4

*Simulated pilot. The findings below illustrate how a low-adoption investigation is run and recorded.*

**Trigger:** in week 4, Pilot 2 made 16% of lookups through Kamilisha against 56% in Pilot 1, and 59% of Pilot 2's lookups in weeks 1 to 4 still went to colleagues. The pilot plan calls for a review when a branch is far below the other.

**Method:** half a day in the branch: observed the counter for two hours, spoke with 6 officers, the supervisor, the branch manager and both champions, and compared the question log by week.

## Findings

| # | Finding | Evidence | Type |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | The supervisor kept answering procedure questions at the counter, so asking a person stayed the quickest habit | Observed 9 questions to the supervisor in two hours; the supervisor had not been briefed | Leadership |
| 2 | Counter PCs needed a separate login to open Kamilisha, taking about a minute | Timed at three counters | Access |
| 3 | **Unsafe workaround:** officers shared screenshots of Kamilisha answers in the branch WhatsApp group. Screenshots go out of date when a circular changes and could capture customer details | Seen on two phones; 14 screenshots in the group | Risk |
| 4 | One of the two champions was on leave in weeks 2 and 3 | Leave roster | Support |

## Actions

| # | Action | Owner | By |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Supervisor briefing; supervisor answers "What did Kamilisha say?" before giving an answer | Branch manager | Week 5 |
| 2 | Single sign-on shortcut on every counter PC | Data Management Office | Week 5 |
| 3 | Remind staff of the rule on customer data in messaging apps; ask the group to delete screenshots; champions post a weekly "ask Kamilisha for the current answer" tip instead. Reported to Compliance | Branch manager, Compliance | Week 5 |
| 4 | Name a back-up champion | Branch manager | Week 5 |

## Result

Pilot 2 adoption rose from 16% in week 4 to 67% in week 8, passing the 60% target. No screenshots were seen at the week 8 check.

## Watch item

Pilot 1 fell from 72% in week 7 to 58% in week 8. Check in the extension whether month-end queues push staff back to habit, and whether champions need a reminder.
