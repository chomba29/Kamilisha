# Communications plan

Co-owned with HR Internal Communications. Messages go out in English and Swahili.

## Key messages

1. **Why:** circulars change and it is hard to find the current rule quickly. Kamilisha gives the current steps in seconds, with the source.
2. **What it is not:** it does not approve anything, replace the intranet, or replace your judgement. Every control still applies.
3. **Not monitoring:** questions are used to improve circulars and training, never to rate individual staff.
4. **Your feedback counts:** press "Wrong or unclear" and the procedure owner fixes it within 2 working days.
