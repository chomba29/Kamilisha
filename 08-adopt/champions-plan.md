# Champions plan

## Who

Two champions per pilot branch: one customer service officer and one relationship officer, chosen by the branch manager for being trusted by colleagues, not for seniority.

## What they do

| When | Activity | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Before go-live | 1-hour champion session with the AI Business Analyst; try every demo scenario | 1 hour |
| Week 1 | On the floor helping colleagues ask their first questions | 30 minutes a day |
| Weeks 1 to 8 | Collect feedback at the morning huddle; share one tip a week | 10 minutes a day |
| Weekly | 20-minute call with the AI Business Analyst and the other branch's champions | 20 minutes |

## What they get

Early access, recognition in the branch manager's reports and pilot results, and a direct line to the procedure owners.

## Watch-outs

- Champions answer "how do I use it", not "what is the procedure": procedure questions go to Kamilisha or the supervisor.
- If a champion hears that staff avoid the tool, they report it the same week (risk R07).
