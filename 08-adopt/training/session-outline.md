# 30-minute training session

**Audience:** all pilot branch staff · **Run by:** training unit with the branch champions · **Format:** at the branch, on the staff's own PCs

| Minutes | Topic | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 to 5 | Why: the circular changed, the colleague didn't know. Two officers, two answers | Story: the card-blocking form |
| 5 to 12 | Ask your first question; read the steps, the CONTROL badges, the source and the "changed on" flag | Everyone asks "Customer lost their card, what do I do?" |
| 12 to 17 | Ask in Swahili; open the circular's own wording on a CONTROL step | Everyone asks one question in Swahili |
| 17 to 22 | What it will not do: "not found", declined workarounds, no customer details | Try a question outside the library; see a number masked |
| 22 to 27 | When the answer looks wrong: check the source, ask your supervisor, press "Wrong or unclear" | Practise pressing the button |
| 27 to 30 | Questions; hand out the quick guide and the "what it can't do" card | |

**Check before leaving:** each person has asked at least two questions, one in Swahili.
