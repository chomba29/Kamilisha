# Kamilisha quick guide · Mwongozo mfupi

| English | Kiswahili |
| --- | --- |
| **1. Ask** your question in English or Swahili, in your own words. | **1. Uliza** swali lako kwa Kiingereza au Kiswahili, kwa maneno yako mwenyewe. |
| **2. Read** the numbered steps. Steps marked **CONTROL** are mandatory. | **2. Soma** hatua zilizopangwa kwa namba. Hatua zenye alama ya **CONTROL** ni za lazima. |
| **3. Check** the source under the answer: circular, section and page. Open the circular's own wording on any CONTROL step. | **3. Hakiki** chanzo chini ya jibu: waraka, kifungu na ukurasa. Fungua maneno halisi ya waraka kwenye hatua ya CONTROL. |
| **4. Watch** for "Changed on": the rule changed recently, so habit may be out of date. | **4. Angalia** "Changed on": kanuni imebadilika hivi karibuni, hivyo mazoea yanaweza kuwa yamepitwa na wakati. |
| **5. "Not found"** means ask your supervisor. Kamilisha never guesses. | **5. "Haikupatikana"** maana yake muulize msimamizi wako. Kamilisha haibahatishi. |
| **6. Never** type customer names, account, card, phone or ID numbers. | **6. Kamwe** usiandike majina ya wateja, namba za akaunti, kadi, simu au kitambulisho. |
| **7. Wrong or unclear?** Press the button. The procedure owner fixes it within 2 working days. | **7. Jibu si sahihi au halieleweki?** Bonyeza kitufe. Mmiliki wa utaratibu atarekebisha ndani ya siku 2 za kazi. |

Kamilisha advises. You stay responsible, and every control still applies. · Kamilisha inashauri tu. Wewe ndiye mwenye jukumu, na kila udhibiti bado unatumika.
