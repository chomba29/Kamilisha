# What Kamilisha can't do

Print as a card for each counter.

| Kamilisha can't... | So you... |
| --- | --- |
| Approve, authorise or make an exception | Follow the approval rules in the circular; ask your supervisor or branch manager |
| Answer from anything except the current circulars | Get "Not found", then ask your supervisor |
| Help skip, delay or reorder a control step | Complete the control. Kamilisha restates it and the question is reviewed |
| Know anything about a specific customer | Check the customer's records in the bank's systems |
| Take customer details | Never type names, account, card, phone or ID numbers; numbers are removed automatically |
| Replace the circular | Open the cited section when in doubt; the circular wins if they differ |
| Rate or monitor you | Ask freely: questions are counted by branch and procedure, never by name |
