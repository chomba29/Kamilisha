# Stage 8 — Adopt

**Question:** How will staff learn Kamilisha, trust it and use it, and how will the pilot run?

## Summary

Adoption is planned role by role: what changes for each group, who leads it in each branch, what staff are told and when, a 30-minute training session, and a pilot with clear go, adjust or stop rules. Microsoft Copilot is covered too, with a prompt sheet showing where it fits next to Kamilisha.

## Key judgements

- **Trust comes from honesty about limits.** Training spends as long on what Kamilisha can't do as on what it can.
- **"Not monitoring" is a promise kept in the design** (guardrail G-12), not only a message.
- **The pilot has a stop rule.** Adoption below 30% means the benefits case fails, so the pilot says so rather than drifting on.
- **Copilot and Kamilisha do different jobs.** Kamilisha answers from the current circulars; Copilot helps write around them.

## Handover to Stage 9

Stage 9 measures the pilot: adoption, quality and benefits, with evidence kept apart from forecasts and activity.
