# Change impact assessment

What changes for each group when Kamilisha goes live in the two pilot branches, and the support each needs. Impact: High = daily work changes; Medium = some tasks change; Low = awareness only.

| Group | What changes | Impact | Main concern | Support |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tellers and customer service officers | Ask Kamilisha instead of a colleague; check the cited section before acting on a CONTROL step | High | "Will it slow me down at the counter?" "Is it watching me?" | 30-minute training, quick guide, champion on the floor in week 1 |
| Relationship officers | Business account and lending questions answered with the current documents list | Medium | Trust in answers for business customers | Training; demo of the business-account and loan questions |
| Supervisors | Fewer interruptions; still the escalation point for "not found" answers and exceptions | Medium | Losing visibility of what staff ask | Weekly question summary for the branch; supervisor briefing |
| Branch managers | Lead adoption in the branch; approve exceptions as before | Medium | Time for huddles and follow-up | Manager briefing; adoption figures each week |
| Procedure owners (Cards, Operations, Digital, Credit, HR) | Keep their circulars current in the library; answer "Wrong or unclear" within 2 working days | Medium | Extra workload | Library template; feedback queue; upkeep time costed (assumption C05) |
| Training unit | Uses the most-asked questions dashboard to plan training | Medium | Learning a new report | Dashboard walkthrough |
| Back office QA | Same checks; expects fewer returns | Low | None expected | Shown QA results each week |
| Compliance | Reviews declined workaround questions weekly | Low | Volume of reviews | Declined list filtered to genuine workaround attempts (DEF-005 fix) |
| Data Management Office | Runs the service, library sync, monitoring and switch-off | High | Support capacity during the pilot | Named support contact; runbook |

## What does not change

- Every control in the circulars still applies, and the system hard stop for the blocking form stays.
- Staff stay responsible for what they do. Kamilisha advises; it does not approve.
- The intranet stays the official source; Kamilisha cites it.
