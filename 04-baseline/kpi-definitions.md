# KPI definitions

Baseline values are in [baseline-summary.md](baseline-summary.md). Targets are proposed for the eight-week pilot and agreed with the business owner at sign-off. Every target is judged on the change in pilot branches **minus** the change in comparison branches.

## Business outcome KPIs (measured at baseline)

| ID | KPI | Definition | Source | Speed of signal | Pilot target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| K3 | QA return rate | Files returned by QA ÷ files submitted, three procedures | QA return log | Fast (weekly) | 40% lower |
| K3a | QA return rate, card replacement | As K3, card replacement only | QA return log | Fast (weekly) | 50% lower |
| K4 | Blocking form on file | Card replacements with a signed blocking form ÷ all card replacements | QA file check | Fast (weekly) | At least 95%; 100% once the system hard stop is live |
| K1 | Minutes to an answer | Median minutes from question to answer, when a lookup was needed | Timed observation sample | Medium | 1.5 minutes or less |
| K2 | Colleague questions | Questions to colleagues per officer per day, three procedures | Observation sample | Medium | 50% lower |
| K6 | Staff confidence | "I am confident I am using the current procedure", 1 to 5 | Staff survey | Medium | Up 0.8 points |
| K5 | Audit queries | Audit queries on the three procedures per branch per quarter | Internal audit log | Slow (quarterly) | No pilot target; tracked two quarters after rollout |

Order of evidence, as agreed in discovery: QA returns first, then time to answer, then audit queries.

## Adoption and quality KPIs (start with the pilot; no baseline)

| ID | KPI | Definition | Pilot target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| K7 | Adoption | Lookups made through Kamilisha ÷ all lookups needed (tests assumption A06) | 60% by week 8 |
| K8 | Active users | Staff who asked at least one question in the week ÷ staff trained | 70% weekly |
| K9 | Answer quality | Answers marked correct in a weekly sample review by procedure owners | 95% or more |
| K10 | Wrong or unclear rate | Answers flagged "Wrong or unclear" ÷ answers given | 5% or less |
| K11 | Not found rate | "Not found" replies ÷ questions; high values show library gaps | Tracked; no target |
| K12 | Update acknowledgement | Staff who ticked a control-step change within 30 days ÷ staff affected (Stage 2, P1) | 90%; below this triggers Scenario B |

## Activity measures (reported, never counted as benefit)

Training attendance, licences issued, questions asked in total. These show effort, not results.

## Response-time target confirmed

A lookup takes about five minutes at baseline (median), so the proposed 10-second response target in the BRD (NFR-01) is confirmed as a usability bar rather than a benefit driver.
